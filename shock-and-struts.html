Off-Road Suspension

Conquer Every Terrain with Adatt Auto Parts

Off-road adventures put demanding stress on your suspension. Adatt performance shocks and struts help Jeeps, Tacomas and 4WD vehicles take on rough roads with confidence.

Embracing the Adventure

Uneven terrain, rocks, ruts and changing loads require components designed for more than smooth pavement. Choosing the right setup helps your vehicle remain controlled and comfortable.

Understanding the Challenges

Off-road suspension needs to manage repeated impacts while maintaining tire contact and predictable handling.

Shocks

Control spring movement and absorb impacts to maintain tire contact and a stable ride.

Struts

Combine a shock absorber and structural suspension support in one assembly.

Off-Road-Specific Considerations

Vehicle use, lift height, spring rates and terrain all matter when choosing suspension components.
