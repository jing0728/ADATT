About ADATT AUTO

Our goal is simple: provide the best quality and price products to satisfy our customers.

Who we are

ADATT AUTO is an aftermarket auto parts brand serving repair shops, distributors and vehicle owners across North America. We supply replacement ride-control components for light duty vehicles and heavy duty fleets.

We work closely with OE manufacturers. ADATT branded parts are built to the same requirement as the original equipment product — same fit, same form, same function — while factory-direct supply keeps pricing well below the dealer counter.

Every part number in our catalog is validated by year, make, model and engine, so what arrives is what bolts on.

How an ADATT part gets to you

A part only carries the ADATT name after it clears every step below.

1

OE teardown

We measure the original component and document tolerances before tooling begins.

2

Material match

Castings, friction compounds and bearing steel are specified to the OE standard.

3

Sample validation

First articles are test-fitted on the target vehicle before mass production starts.

4

In-line inspection

Dimensional and runout checks run continuously during production, not just at the end.

5

Pre-shipment QC

Every carton is checked for the correct part number, hardware and packaging.

6

Field feedback

Installer reports feed straight back into the next production revision.
