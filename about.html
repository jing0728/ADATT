About Adatt Off-Road Shocks

Engineered for the Way You Drive

Adatt brings together engineers and off-road enthusiasts to design and manufacture shocks, struts, coil spring assemblies and stabilizers for weekend trails and overland adventures.

Our focus is quality, durability, performance and customer service. We use high-grade materials and rigorous testing to build products for Jeep, Toyota, Ford and Chevy trucks and SUVs.
